# Third-party notices

The frozen app trees include pinned copies of third-party libraries where required by their format engines.

- JSZip is bundled inside Documents, Spreadsheets and Presentations.
- Pako is bundled inside Documents and Spreadsheets.

The license texts distributed with the canonical Spreadsheets FINAL package are retained under `licenses/` for suite-level notice. App-private vendor copies are intentionally not deduplicated.
