# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is InkDesk a complete Microsoft Office replacement?
No. It targets focused everyday editing and partial format compatibility.

## Does it require an account or Internet connection?
No account is required, and core workflows are designed to operate locally and offline.

## Are files uploaded?
InkDesk has no project-operated upload backend. Files are processed in the current browser context.

## Are macros executed?
No. Macros, embedded scripts, ActiveX, and remote document instructions are not intentionally executed.

## Which formats are supported?
DOCX, XLSX, BIFF8 XLS import, PPTX and the local PDF Workspace, with the limitations documented in `COMPATIBILITY.md`.
