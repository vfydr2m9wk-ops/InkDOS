# Code of Conduct

InkDOS is a small, open, local-first project. We want everyone who uses it, reports problems, discusses ideas or contributes code to be able to do so without harassment or hostility.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the InkDOS community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences, including different levels of technical experience.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on issues, pull requests and designs.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use InkDOS, including their privacy.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing to those affected and learning from them.
- Writing in plain language, so reports and reviews stay useful to people reading in a second language.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Posting real user documents, screenshots or personal, medical or educational data in public issues or pull requests (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)); use synthetic reproductions instead.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and any public space where someone is officially representing the project.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel: **Security → Report a vulnerability** (https://github.com/vfydr2m9wk-ops/InkDOS/security). Start the title with `Conduct:` so the report is not mistaken for a security issue. Please include links to the relevant comments, issues or pull requests.

All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers respect the privacy and security of the person reporting.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these guidelines to decide on consequences:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior, a private written warning explaining why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or a series of actions, a warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior, a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people, a permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.
