PDF tools Beta

Experimental tools for the web edition. Everything runs in this browser: files, certificates and passwords never leave your device.

No PDF open

Recognises the text of scanned pages and adds an invisible text layer, so the PDF can be searched and copied. The pages look the same.

Draw or choose a signature image, then click on the page where it goes. A visual signature is a picture: for legal validity use the digital signature.

Signs the PDF with your A1 certificate (.pfx/.p12). The signature is added as an incremental update, so earlier signatures stay valid. Place any visual signature first.

Checks that each signature matches the document and that the document was not changed afterwards. The certificate chain is shown but not checked against a trust list in this beta.
